    Captions and the watermark are drawn in Inter, so a short renders the same on a PC that has Inter installed and one that has never heard of it.     The local speech engine (voice input, the wake word). Its own licence text ships inside desktop/bin/whisper/.

    Public domain — no obligations. Used for the YouTube paths the user asks for.
