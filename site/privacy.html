Privacy

Last updated: 4 October 2026. The short version first, then the details.

Before you go live: put your real company name and contact in section 9.

The short version

  • No account. The app doesn't create one, and there's no Soundwave server holding your work.
  • Your videos render on your PC and stay in %APPDATA%\Soundwave AI\ until you upload them somewhere.
  • Your voice is transcribed on your PC by whisper.cpp. Audio isn't uploaded, and "Hey Soundwave" throws away everything that isn't the phrase.
  • Four things do leave your machine, only when you use them: narration text (Microsoft's voice service), your conversation (Google Gemini, with your own key), the pages the agent reads for you, and payments (Stripe).
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

1. Nothing is uploaded by default

Soundwave AI is a desktop app. Projects, uploads, rendered MP4s, notes, memory, settings, channel watch lists and your API key live in %APPDATA%\Soundwave AI\ on your own computer. We don't have a copy, because there's nowhere to put one — the app doesn't talk to a server of ours.

2. What leaves your PC, and when

  • Narration (Microsoft's neural voice service). When the agent speaks or narrates a video, the text to be spoken is sent to Microsoft's Edge speech service, which returns audio. Nothing else about you goes with it, and no key or account is used.
  • Your conversation (Google Gemini). With an API key set, the messages in your conversation, the memory notes the agent keeps and its tool results are sent to Google to produce the reply, the script or a research summary. The key is yours and stays on your PC. Without a key, this doesn't happen at all — the app falls back to built-in scripts.
  • Pages and videos you ask it to read. Reading a YouTube transcript or a web page fetches that page from your PC. If a page can't be read directly, the app may fetch it through a reader service (r.jina.ai by default) — which means that page's address goes to it. The app's own guide says when that happens, and you can run the optional local reader (scrapling/) instead so it never does.
  • Your screen, when you ask. "What does this error say?" photographs the screen you're on and sends that image to Gemini for the answer. It isn't written to disk.
  • YouTube, if you connect it. Connecting uses Google's OAuth with the app's own client; the token is stored on your PC. It's used to read your channel stats and, when you press the button, to upload a Short you made.
  • Weather and research. The morning briefing asks Open-Meteo for the weather forecast of the city you configured, and may search Google News, GitHub and Hacker News for your topics.

3. Speech, alarms and the microphone

Voice input is recorded only while the microphone is on (the mic button, the voice bar, or the wake phrase listener) and is transcribed locally by whisper.cpp with the model that ships in the installer. The audio is not uploaded and not kept. The wake listener discards every burst that isn't "Hey Soundwave" on the spot, and pauses itself while the app is already recording or speaking. Alarms and notifications are set on your phone through the paired app, not through us.

4. This website

The site you're reading is static: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising. Two things worth naming honestly: the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page requests them, and if you pay, Stripe receives the payment details needed to process the charge (see below). Server logs, if your host keeps them, contain the usual request data — IP, user agent, time — and are used only to keep the site up.

5. Payments

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Your card number goes from your browser to Stripe and never to us — we only receive the email address and plan from the completed checkout, in order to recognise your purchase. Stripe's own privacy policy governs what it keeps.

6. If you email us

We keep support threads so we can remember what we told you. Ask us to delete a thread and we will, unless we're required to keep an invoice or similar record for tax purposes.

7. Children

The app isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has used it, email us and we'll help you remove the data — the app's data lives on your own computer, so uninstalling and deleting %APPDATA%\Soundwave AI\ removes all of it.

8. Your rights

Because we don't hold your content, most requests are already satisfied by your own machine. For anything we do hold — a support thread, a purchase record — you can ask to see it, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can ask us to port it elsewhere. You also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority. Requests go to support, answered within 30 days.
