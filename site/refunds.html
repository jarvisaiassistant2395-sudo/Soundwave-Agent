Refunds & cancellations

Last updated: 4 October 2026. Simple rules, in plain words.

Decide your own numbers before launch. The window below (14 days) is the usual European standard and is generous enough to be a selling point — change it to whatever you will actually honour, and make sure the Terms match.

14-day money-back guarantee

If a paid plan isn't for you, email support within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full — to the same card, no argument. That covers the plan you started with. It's a guarantee, not store credit: the money comes back.

  • Tell us the email address you paid with (or the Stripe receipt number).
  • Refunds go back through Stripe and usually land within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • You don't have to explain why. If you tell us anyway, we'll use it to fix the right thing.

Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time from the link in your Stripe receipt email — no call, no form, no retention gauntlet.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then drops to the free plan's limits.
  • Your projects, uploads and settings stay on your PC. Nothing is deleted when you downgrade.
  • Turning off auto-renew is the same thing as cancelling: no further charges, no lost work.

After the 14 days

We don't pro-rate partial months. If something is genuinely broken and we can't fix it for you, write to us anyway — we'd rather refund an unhappy customer than keep one. Charges we can't refund are the ones we'd be breaking the law by refunding, and there aren't many of those.

Billing mistakes

Double-charged, charged after cancelling, or charged in a currency you didn't expect? Email us within 60 days of the charge with your receipt and we'll correct it. Talk to us before opening a chargeback — a bank dispute takes weeks and starts with your plan being suspended, and we can usually fix the problem the same day.

Your statutory rights

If you're a consumer in the EU, UK, Serbia or anywhere else with consumer protections, you have rights that these rules can't reduce — including withdrawal rights for digital content. Where we're required to do better than the 14-day guarantee above, the law wins, and you only need to ask. Digital content is delivered immediately when you buy, which is why we ask you to accept delivery at checkout; the guarantee above is our answer to that, and it's deliberately simpler than the law requires.

Not satisfied with a refund decision?

Ask for it to be looked at again — say so in the email and a different person will review it. If that doesn't settle it and you're in the EU, you can use your national consumer arbitration body. Our details for that are on the Terms page.
